Design system · derived from mood 1d

Tile House 牌

A digit is a fired ceramic tile with weight, an engraved face, and a history of use. You own exactly ten sockets and never more. Spending a tile leaves a scar in the rack; the rack filling up is the only clock this game has. Calm materials, tense arithmetic.

The rack — ten sockets

5 × 2, fixed forever; the board's only pressure gauge
6 / 10 · comfortable

Empty sockets are scars, not gaps. Tiles never re-flow — a digit keeps its socket for the whole run.
