# 2026-09-05 — M5.5c, and what the focus ring actually measures

Task T37. The gate: *"Both primitives wired at every call site; accessible names
unchanged; the focus indicator ≥ `3:1` on ceramic as well as felt; suite green."*
CI proves three of those four and is silent on the one that matters, so this is the
measured record.

All readings from `/gallery.html` at `dpr: 1`, screenshots diffed pixel by pixel:
the indicator's colour read from the focused image, its backdrop from the unfocused
image at the same coordinates. No token values, no computed styles.

## The two-tone ring works, and neither tone would have

| Surface | gold `#e8cd85` | dark `#16352b` | verdict |
|---|---|---|---|
| rack tile, ceramic `#f4ebd8` | **1.31** | **11.23** | passes on the dark line |
| answer slot tile, ceramic `#f4ead8` | **1.30** | **11.16** | passes on the dark line |
| Submit, vermilion `#b5432f` | **3.55** | 2.41 | passes on the gold line |
| Clear, raised felt `#1b4436` | **7.01** | 1.22 | passes on the gold line |
| Copy Result, ghost over `#071711` | **11.85** | 1.38 | passes on the gold line |

The two tones read against each other at **8.56:1** on every surface, so the bezel
never collapses into one band.

This is the whole argument for carrying two tones. Gold alone fails ceramic at
`1.31`; the dark line alone fails felt at `1.22` and vermilion at `2.41`. Every
surface is cleared by whichever line suits it, and no single-tone ring clears them
all. `elevation.css` predicted `8.56` between the tones and got it exactly.

Two of the appearances `T37` names carry no reading, and neither can:

| Appearance | Reading |
|---|---|
| reward tile, `sm`, in `FeedbackPanel` | **not measurable — cannot receive focus.** It is rendered without `onClick`, so `Tile` gives it no button element and no `button` role. Nothing focusable, so no indicator to measure. |
| `ActionButton` disabled | **not measurable — cannot receive focus.** A disabled `<button>` is removed from the tab order and does not match `:focus-visible`. Its own gate is that it draws *no* edge, which the suite asserts. |

Recorded as unmeasured rather than passed. Both become measurable the moment a
later phase gives an `sm` tile an `onClick` or makes a disabled control
focusable, and both would then need a reading — the `sm` case doubly so, since
it is under the target minimum.

Where prediction and measurement diverged, the measurement was right and for a
reason worth keeping: the design estimated gold-on-ceramic at `1.10` against
`--clay-200`, but the tile face is a gradient and the ring lands nearer
`--clay-050`, so the real figure is `1.31`. Same conclusion, different number. The
ghost boundary was computed at `1.31` against `--felt-700` and measures `1.27` —
the game-over panel sits on `--felt-900`, not the felt the arithmetic assumed.

## The ring composes onto the edge — proved below the tile, not in the shadow list

`M5.5b`'s defect was structural: a focus `box-shadow` declared beside an elevation
`box-shadow` replaces it. Reading the column below a focused rack tile:

```
 dy   unfocused   focused
 -2   #e9dbc0     #e8cd85    <- ring drew inside the face
 -1   #e9dbc0     #e8cd85
 +0   #b3a184     #b3a184    <- --clay-400, the tile's 5px thickness
 +4   #b3a184     #b3a184       identical, so the edge survived focus
 +5   #05100c     #05100c
```

The edge is byte-identical focused and unfocused. That is the composition, read
from pixels rather than from a `box-shadow` string that could contain both values
and still paint one.

## The ring is withheld on click

Clicked with a real pointer: `:focus` true, `:focus-visible` **false**, and the
sixteen pixels across the tile's left edge are byte-identical to the unfocused
frame. Keyboard focus on the same tile draws `#e8cd85, #e8cd85, #16352b`.

This is the half both reference implementations get wrong — `Tile.jsx` and
`ActionButton.jsx` wire the ring through `onFocus`/`onBlur`, which fire on
click-focus too. A port that regressed to handlers would pass every other reading
in this document.

## Three ways the measurement lied before it told the truth

Recorded because each cost real time and each would have produced a confident
wrong number.

**`matches(':active')` is always false.** `:active` is dynamic — it holds only
while the element is pressed, so matching a live element against an `:active`
selector reports "no press rule" for every control in the app. Strip the
pseudo-class and match the static part.

**`matches(':focus-visible')` after a click can be true.** Clicking an element
that was *already* focused does not move focus, so Chrome keeps the previous
modality. The clean test needs a fresh load and a control that was not focused
first — otherwise the ring appears to fire on click when it does not.

**Cropping to the border box misses an outset indicator.** The first pass reported
"no visible focus indicator" on `LanguageToggle`. Its ring is the global
`:focus-visible` outline, drawn *outside* the box; the crop began exactly at the
edge. Scanning from `8px` outside inward found it at offsets `-4..-3`, gold on the
surround at **11.85:1**. `T32` warned that the band is the thing to find first;
this is what ignoring that looks like.

A fourth, from the same session and the same family: a guard test written for the
press composition passed while the invariant was broken, because its selector
filter excluded exactly the rules it was meant to check. It was only caught by
deliberately re-introducing the defect to see whether the test could fail. A check
that has never failed has not been tested.

## Carried forward

- **`LanguageToggle` is `M5.5d`'s and its ring passes only because it is outset.**
  Gold at `11.85:1` on the surround. The moment `M5.5d` gives it the inset bezel,
  the backdrop becomes the gold active segment, where D1 measured `1.51`. The dark
  line is what will carry it — the same way ceramic is carried here.
- **The ghost boundary is `1.27:1` and stays.** Accepted knowingly, like the
  vermilion primary. Its label carries identification at `8.16:1`, so §1.12 is
  satisfied by the label rather than the border. A disabled button wears the same
  hairline over the same transparent fill; today nothing renders a disabled ghost,
  so the collision is latent.
- **Old rules are gone, not shadowed.** `.tile`, `.reward` and the filled `.slot`
  ceramic rules no longer exist; `FeedbackPanel`'s `.reward` is pure layout now,
  and `AnswerSlots`' `.slot` is the empty socket the primitive deliberately does
  not collapse.
- **The `sm` tile is `30 × 38`**, under the `44 × 44` target minimum. Safe only
  while its sole call site is roleless. Any phase that gives an `sm` tile an
  `onClick` breaks §1.12.
