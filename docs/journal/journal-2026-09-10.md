# 2026-09-10 — M5.5g opens: two amendments, and a gate that is not re-scored

`T57`. Docs only. The phase's five later tasks are all scoped by the roadmap row
this one writes, so it lands first and alone.

## Amendment 1 — `10i` is struck, and the inventory is fifteen

§1.12 permits *the named inventory in `docs/design-system/decisions.md`* and
says outright that extending the inventory amends the section. Reducing it is
the same act, so it gets the same treatment.

`10i` — the game-over table sweep — needs tiles on the rack at game over, and
the game-over screen has no rack. Giving it one is new product surface and a
`product.md` §1.10 amendment. The question underneath it is a product question,
not a motion one: *should the game-over screen show the final rack at all?*
Ori's answer is no. So the moment has nothing to play on.

The row follows `10d`'s form rather than being deleted. The inventory records
what was considered, not only what shipped — a deleted row leaves the next
reader to rediscover the same dead end.

**The strike touches no code.** Verified rather than assumed:
`keyframes.css` defines fifteen `@keyframes` and none of them is a sweep. The
shape exists only as `sb-sweep` in the storyboard, which was never ported. Had
one been authored, the ruling was that it stays defined and unused on
`oz-rise-ready`'s precedent — that branch was not needed.

## The consequence I am deliberately not taking

With `10i` struck, `M5.5f`'s exit gate — *every moment in the §1.12 inventory
implemented* — reads true: fifteen moments, fifteen implemented. It would take
one line to make that phase's record look clean.

It is not being taken. **A gate is scored against the standard in force when the
phase closes**, not against a later amendment that happens to flatter it.
`M5.5f` shipped short of its gate as the gate stood on the day, `M5.5b` did the
same before it, and both are recorded that way. The roadmap paragraph declaring
the miss stays verbatim; the new paragraph sits beneath it and says why the
arithmetic changed without changing the score.

The tidy table is worth less than the record. A roadmap that quietly re-scores
closed phases against amended standards stops being evidence of anything.

## Amendment 2 — the `M5.5g` row now describes the phase it names

The old gate was written before the phase inherited a docket:

> Gallery covers hover, focus-visible, disabled and reduced-motion; §8.5 walked
> with measured evidence at 320px

Both clauses survive. What joined them: every gallery state renders what its
name claims (#105 — three of them do not), the 320px walk happens **in a real
top-level viewport** rather than an iframe (#85), reduced motion is read **per
moment and in both directions**, no rack badge overlaps the engraved digit in
either locale or either font face (#84), and `docs/design-system/` holds only
what the product still reads (#58).

Two of those are stricter than what `T56` could deliver, on purpose. `T56`'s
320px readings were clean at all seven widths #85 names, but they were taken in
a fixed-width iframe because a backgrounded automation tab refuses a window
resize — and whether an iframe's scrollbar behaves as a top-level window's is
the whole question #85 turns on. Its reduced-motion reading argued the general
case from `global.css`'s single `!important` sweep after measuring four
representative properties. Both arguments are sound and neither is a
measurement. `T58`'s fixtures are what make the measurement possible, which is
why it blocks `T62`.

## Three issues left off the docket, with a reason each

The milestone plan put #64, #77 and #31 on this phase. They are off it, and the
roadmap says so rather than letting the plan and the milestone disagree
silently — an unstated exclusion is indistinguishable from an oversight.

- **#64** — duplicate `.woff` files in `dist/`. A build-output defect; nothing
  in the gate touches the bundle.
- **#77** — Stylelint. Its own title says *once M5.5 settles*, and adding a
  linter mid-verification churns every stylesheet the phase exists to hold
  still.
- **#31** — post-release tidy. Predates `M5.5` and is labelled as such.

**#58 stays in.** It was blocked only on `M5.5f` still reading the storyboard
canvases, and that block died with the merge.
