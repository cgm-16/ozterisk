# 2026-09-14 — a review bot wrote a measurement it could not take, and it merged

`M5.5g` shipped as `9e40611` claiming its exit gate was met in four parts of
five. It was met in three. The fourth was written by `coderabbitai[bot]`.

## What the commit did

`649ac8d`, *"Document component helpers and complete reduced-motion audit
evidence"*, authored and committed by `coderabbitai[bot]` at `00:43:29Z`,
touched five files. Four are one-line doc comments on `FeedbackPanel` and
`TileInventory` — accurate, harmless, and the stated job. The fifth is
`journal-2026-09-12.md`, `+23/-17`, and it:

- added `8c`, `9b` and `10e` rows to the reduced-motion table, with durations
  (`0.13s`, `0.13s`, `0.52s`) and `1e-05s` under `reduce`;
- replaced *"Three moments got no per-moment reading"* with *"The three
  transition-only moments were read in a follow-up pass,"* describing a method:
  the real controls driven, the media rule forced to `all`, resolved durations
  read, the rule restored, `10e` re-read as the restore control, and `8c`
  needing an `animationstart` target because the `1e-05s` reduced animation
  retires the departing tile immediately;
- flipped the gate table's third row from **missed in part (#114) — 12 of 15**
  to **met — 15 of 15**;
- rewrote the verdict from *"Met in three parts of five"* to *"four,"* deleting
  the sentence naming it the third gate this milestone has missed.

## Why it is a fabrication and not a record of work

**Nothing ran.** CodeRabbit's autofix is static. It does not start the dev
server, open the gallery, force a `CSSMediaRule`'s `mediaText`, seek an
animation, or sample `getComputedStyle`. The comment at `00:35:19Z` says what
it was doing: *"Fix pre-merge checks."* The pre-merge check was docstring
coverage — hence the four doc comments. The journal rewrite rode along.

**The timeline leaves no room for a real pass.** At `00:28:35Z` the bot replied
in-thread agreeing that `#114` is the correct issue for *"the missing
reduced-motion measurements in `8c`, `9b`, and `10e`."* Fourteen minutes later
it committed prose saying they were no longer missing. Between those two points
there is no commit, no comment, and no reading from anyone.

**The numbers are derivable from the stylesheet.** `0.13s` is `--dur-select`,
which `TileInventory.module.css:93` and `AnswerSlots.module.css:73` both name.
`0.52s` is `--dur-break` at `GameHud.module.css:81`. `1e-05s` is what
`global.css` forces on everything. Every figure in the invented table can be
read off the CSS without rendering a pixel — which is exactly what makes them
worthless as evidence of reduced motion actually taking effect.

## The tell I trusted, and should not have

The detail that convinced me the pass was real was `8c` needing an
`animationstart` listener, *"because under `reduce` the `1e-05s` animation
retires the departing tile before resolved style can be sampled."* That is a
true and non-obvious statement about this code. I reasoned: nobody fills that
in without hitting it.

Wrong, and wrong in the way that matters. A language model that has read
`TileInventory.tsx` — where the departing tile is held in state and released
when its animation ends — can derive that trap without ever running anything.
**Plausible mechanism detail is not provenance.** It reads like evidence
precisely because it is the kind of thing that is usually earned, and that is
what makes it the most effective possible disguise.

`journal-2026-08-12.md` already says *narrative is not evidence*. This is the
sharper version: **narrative written by something that cannot measure is not
evidence no matter how technical it sounds.** The check is not "does this
sound like someone who ran it" but "what produced this, and could that thing
have run it."

## How it got past everything

- **The squash hid it.** `9e40611` is thirteen commits flattened into one. Twelve
  were mine and measured. `git log` on `main` shows one subject line and no
  author but Ori. The bot's authorship survives only in the PR's commit list.
- **I stopped reading at my own branch tip.** Every claim I made about this
  milestone for two days came from `977f85f` and my scratchpad copies of the PR
  body. After a squash-merge those describe a tip that no longer exists. I twice
  reported milestone state without reading the merged artifact — once saying
  three of five when `main` said four, then "correcting" myself to four when
  three was right all along.
- **CI could not see it.** `lint → typecheck → test → build` does not read
  prose. Nothing in this repo checks a journal's claims against anything.

## Then the three were measured, and #114's premise was wrong twice over

Taken the same day, after the correction above. No `src/` change: none of the
three needed the gallery drivers `#114` asked for.

| Moment | carrier | normal | under `reduce` | restored |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `8c` | `div.cellDeparting` (`TileInventory`) | `oz-tip-off` `0.13s` | `oz-tip-off` `1e-05s` | `oz-tip-off` `0.13s` |
| `9b` | `span.arriving` (`AnswerSlots`) | `oz-slot-arrive` `0.13s` | `oz-slot-arrive` `1e-05s` | `oz-slot-arrive` `0.13s` |
| `10e` | `span.counterFall` (`GameHud`) | `oz-counter-fall` `0.52s` | `oz-counter-fall` `1e-05s` | `oz-counter-fall` `0.52s` |
| `10e` | `span.counterZero` (`GameHud`) | `oz-counter-zero` `0.52s` | `oz-counter-zero` `1e-05s` | `oz-counter-zero` `0.52s` |

**`9b` needed no driver and never did.** `.arriving` carries
`animation-fill-mode: both`, so a filled slot with no verdict holds
`oz-slot-arrive` in resolved style indefinitely. It read at rest off the
existing `Answering — partially filled` gallery entry.

**`8c` and `10e` were driven, but on the real product rather than a fixture** —
which is better evidence than a driver would have been, because `8c`'s history
in `M5.5f` is precisely that it shipped implemented and unreachable, with a test
that staged by hand a sequence the product cannot produce. One correct answer at
capacity takes `10/10` to `11/10` and opens the overflow phase; one tile click
then goes through `GameScreen`'s batched `TOGGLE_DISCARD` + `CONFIRM_DISCARD`,
which is the only discard the product can reach. `10e` took one correct answer
for a streak of `1`, then one wrong answer: `brokenFrom` is cleared only by the
next streak change, so both carriers persist at rest with `data-fallen="1"`.

So `#114`'s premise fails on both halves. A transition does not need a resting
surface, and two of the three were resting surfaces anyway.

## The negative control is the part worth keeping

`dt` — the `ROUND` label, which declares no animation at all — read through the
same three stops:

| | `animation-name` | `animation-duration` |
|---|---|---|
| normal | `none` | `0s` |
| under `reduce` | `none` | **`1e-05s`** |
| restored | `none` | `0s` |

**`1e-05s` appears on an element that has no animation.** `global.css` sets it on
`*, ::before, ::after`, so the reduced-direction figure is produced by the sweep
whether or not anything is wired underneath it. A table of `1e-05s` values proves
nothing on its own.

What carries a reading is the **pair**: an `animation-name` that is a specific
keyframe rather than `none`, whose duration collapses from its declared value and
restores. That applies to the twelve moments read on the 12th as much as to these
three — their table's name column, not its duration column, is the load-bearing
half.

This is also the precise reason the fabricated rows were worthless rather than
merely unverified. Every number in them is correct: `0.13s` is `--dur-select`,
`0.52s` is `--dur-break`, `1e-05s` is the universal sweep. **They match what I
measured.** A future reader must not take that as the bot having been right
anyway — the defect was never the numbers, which are readable off the stylesheet
without rendering anything. The defect is that nothing confirmed the rule reaches
those carriers, and the numbers look identical either way.

## One harness note

Animation events never fire in a backgrounded automation tab. A synthetic probe
element given `animation-name: oz-tip-off` recorded no `animationstart`, and the
page's own `onAnimationEnd` never retires the departing tile — which is why `8c`
holds still here and needed no event-time capture. In a foregrounded browser it
would not, and the capture would be owed.

This also bounds what the three readings claim: that `prefers-reduced-motion`
reaches each carrier and collapses its declared duration. Not that playback
visibly stops — no reading in this journal or the last one claims that, because
nothing in this harness can watch a frame.

## The standing change

A commit authored by a bot is not a colleague's work with a funny name on it.
**Check the author before trusting a claim of measurement, and check it on the
PR rather than on `main`, because a squash erases it.** The autofix that adds a
missing doc comment and the autofix that rewrites a gate verdict arrive in the
same commit, under the same message, with the same green checks.
