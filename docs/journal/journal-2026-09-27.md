# 2026-09-27 — Rack steps: a device reading, an experiment, and a pick

## #159: the 15 and 10 thresholds were made for five columns

Ori played Classic on an **iPhone 13 mini** (iOS Safari, 375px CSS width, a rack
container of about 351px, so the wide 7-column small size). The step from 15 to
10 felt awkward: a whole row sits empty before the rack drops to ten. The code
explains it. 15 and 10 are whole rows only at five columns, but the mid size was
6 × 3, a fixed 18 cells. So at 12 and 11 its third row was all plugs.

## The experiment (#177, closed unmerged)

A throwaway branch switched schemes by `?rack=`. `rack-run.mjs`, a headless
Chromium driver kept in the session scratchpad and not the repo, played real
Classic runs at 320, 375, 402 and 430px, and read the rack after every
submission. Scheme A reproduced the predicted all-plug rows before anything
changed, so the instrument could fail.

| Scheme | All-plug rows | Steps (submission) |
|---|---|---|
| A, today | 12, 11 at every width; also 18–16 at 320 | 10, 20 |
| B, 5 × 3 mid | none from 375 up; 18–16 at 320 | 10, 20 |
| C, whole rows only | none | 10, 16, 20, plus 4 at 320 |

**Ori picked B.** C's rack shrinks at 12, then grows at 10. The 320px row is
accepted, since those phones are rare. T82 builds it, and `M6·2` retires with it:
no size below twenty now starts with a plug.

## Also measured, and not taken

- **Overflow in the prompt.** Seating the tile past capacity inside the discard
  prompt, instead of on the rail, took the overflow screen from +87px over
  feedback to +8px in Endless, and from +78px to −1px in Classic (375 × 629).
  Ori judged it not needed yet. Nobody judged on a device how its longer drop,
  from the prompt into the freed seat, reads. If it is revisited, start there.
- **The screen scrolls on small phones in every phase.** On production, 375px
  wide, page heights are: title 756, answering 578–591, feedback 709–741,
  overflow 796–800. An iPhone SE (667px screen, about 548 visible with Safari's
  bars) scrolls even while answering. That's a separate job from the rack, and
  no issue is filed yet.
- **Ori's first idea for the overflow tile, seating it on a sealed plug,** was
  never built. It has no plug to sit on in Endless, at the first round of 15
  and 10, or for a second excess tile with one plug. That would take a rail
  fallback, which means two looks for one moment.
