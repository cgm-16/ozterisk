# 2026-10-18 — Ten directions for a third mode

Ten Opus agents each researched one direction for a new mode from a shared
brief: Market, Factor, Puzzle, Table for Two, Card, Riichi, Kiln, Well, Four
Winds and Dojo. The drafts and their index are in `docs/research/game-modes/`.
They are research, not canonical; nothing in `docs/spec/` changed.

## Found, and deliberately not fixed

The drafts' simulations ran on the real `src/game` modules. Their results
disagree in four ways with the economy model in `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-08-09-endless-mode-polish-design.md`
§1.2 and `src/test/economy.ts`. No dial moves on research alone, so each is
recorded here instead:

- **The miss cost depends on the hand.** The model prices a miss at the
  pool-average 1.71 tiles. At a full hand, misses skew to two-digit products and
  cost about 1.81, so drift crosses zero near b ≈ 0.644, not 0.631. Market
  showed this analytically and its engine reproduced it, and I recomputed 1.806
  by hand. The guard therefore errs safe.
- **The ceiling leaks.** A full rack above the cliff does not lock runs in,
  because overflow takes the +1 while a miss still costs. Endless's full-rack b′
  is 0.71, yet runs end in about 20 rounds. Endless rules at capacity 20
  averaged 174 rounds, and no run reached 1,000 (Kiln; Factor). Lock-in does
  occur when choice is free: in Market's free row, 98% of runs passed 100
  submissions.
- **"~22 rounds" is not reproduced.** Bots play Endless for 13.1 rounds with the
  kind bias off and about 18–20 with it on (Table for Two, Factor, Riichi,
  Kiln). Table for Two reads the spec's figure as a linear 10 ÷ 0.41, which
  ignores b falling as the hand shrinks; that reading is its guess, not a
  measurement.
- **Bots differ by up to 17 points on identical rules.** On faceless Classic,
  the T84 bot wins 33.0% and Card's bot 50.2%. Every simulated figure is a
  comparison between variants, as journal 2026-09-27 already warned.

Market asks whether `balance.test.ts`, which is analytic by design, should admit
a simulated guard for a mode with a second state variable. That is Ori's call.

## A correction to the brief

The brief described Classic as a two-wind half-game. Four Winds §1 corrects it
from `decisions.md`: the 24 Sep calls dropped the wind tile, and the East/South
acts were withdrawn because play churns the opening hand.

## Committing the sims

On 2026-10-19 Ori chose to commit the drafts' scripts and outputs (each draft's
`sims/` folder, plus `_runner/`). The T84 work did not commit its script; this
pool does, because every number in it comes from them. The scripts import the
live `src/game`, so they describe 4d1259e and will drift as the game changes. The
re-run sample and its results are listed in the index under "How this was made".
